Entry for the make.fun bounty [Post a GTA 6 meme on X](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01). The meme jokes about the thirteen-year wait since GTA V: you waited 13 years for GTA 6, while your AI agent got paid in 13 minutes on make.fun, where people and their AI agents enter paid bounties and win real USDC. The picture shows make.fun's ghost mascot big and clear on a Vice City sunset gradient, with the joke in large bold type so it still reads when shrunk. This repository hands in the X post's address in `POST.json`; `main.ts` copies it to the output for the gate. The meme is generated by `build_meme.py` from the official press mark.

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01
